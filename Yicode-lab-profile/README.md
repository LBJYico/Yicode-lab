# Yicode Lab

**AI and intelligent sensing for agricultural and biological environmental systems.**

My research focuses on reliable computational methods for sensing-rich agricultural and biological environments, including:

- Physics-informed and trustworthy modeling
- Spatiotemporal data analysis and forecasting
- Computer-vision-based phenotyping

I aim to connect sound modeling with practical sensing and decision support in agriculture and biological environmental systems.
